Ontonomy

Tools for people who write their own story. I meet someone i write a story. I read a book i write a story. I have an idea a diagnosis a family tree a grocery list a project i write a story

Evenor is the app. Stories apear as sentences in a book. I can search across everything and write anew. It runs in browsers, personal computers and smartphones.

A mind is a folder of stories. I can see how it changed over time, share it with others. One for thesongs i wrote, one for my roleplaying campaign, one for family history.

csvs is the standard. Every mind is a handful of text files where two columns are separate by a comma. I can open them in Notepad, Excel, Emacs or in Evenor.
