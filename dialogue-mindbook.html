SOFIA
Can you describe your world?

ANTIPHON
Barely, with a lot of text. Granma was born in 1925. Father was born to granma in 1950. Mother was born in 1951. My brother was born to father and mother in 1987. I was born to them in 1990. I find it hard to explain the larger family without an interface.

SOFIA
So what, do you use social networks or genealogy software that have already decided how to show this for you?

ANTIPHON
Nah, I don't like that they decide for me. And when they change the interface, or close down, I always end up with the short end of the stick. I decide for myself what I want to speak about.

Father was born in 1950
I was born to father and mother in 1990.

SOFIA
So what, do you just write prose, like a lot of notes?

ANTIPHON
Nah, each event has prose and some details, so 1950 is a date, father is a name of that event, and mother is the name of some other event. I can hide prose under "is...", details under "with..." and lists under "and...". It reads like a sentence. I will show the type of each thing in brackets for now.

father(event) is: Father was born(prose) with: 1950(date)
me(event) is: I was born(prose) with: 1990(date) father(name) and: mother(name)
// same hidden
me(event) is... with...
granma-story(event) is: Granma told me about the winter of 1943. She said the river froze.(prose) with: 2023-05-17(date) granma(name)

SOFIA
So you have a list of these sentences.

ANTIPHON
Nah, I need to connect them to each other. I put one event in the center, everything it references on one side, and everything that references it to the other. I can click on father and see father's center instead.

>>father is... with: 1950 .
>>mother is... with: 1951 .
me is: I was born with: 1990 .
// choose father
>>granma is... with: 1925 .
father is... with: 1950 .
>>me is... with... .
>>brother is... with... .

So can you describe your world?

With this book, I can.
